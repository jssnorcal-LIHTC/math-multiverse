# Vault of Ages (G6, English): Provenance

Every figure in this pack is an ORIGINAL BUILT GRAPHIC. Nothing here is photographed, scanned,
traced or sourced from a third party, so no licence question arises and none of the sourcing
rules that govern the science and history packs apply.

What DOES govern this pack is transcription. Each figure is generated deterministically from a
`dataTable` committed inside `packs/vault-of-ages-g6.json`, and every label in that table is a
verbatim substring of the passage the figure illustrates. That is not a convention: it is
enforced by `tests/figure-fidelity.js`, which fails the build for any label it cannot find in
the named passage, for any figure whose committed drawing omits a label its table declares, and
for any caption that states a number or an exclusivity the passage does not.

`tests/figure-derive.js` separately regenerates every SVG below from its own `dataTable` and
byte-compares it against the committed file, so a drawing and the data behind it cannot drift
apart unnoticed.
